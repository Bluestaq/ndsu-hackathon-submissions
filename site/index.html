Agenda

  1. Before the event

    Get ready

    Register with Bluestaq University Programs. Have a GitHub account, Git, and Python 3.11+ ready. Setup steps.

  2. Friday 4:00 pm CT · Hour 0

    Kickoff and opening remarks

    Welcome from Bluestaq, “Food security is national security,” and the challenge. The dataset is released. Film and slides.

  3. Friday 4–5 pm · Hour 0–1

    First working result

    Download the data, run the baseline, validate it, and inspect a labeled episode.

  4. Friday 5 pm – Saturday 4 pm · Hours 1–24

    Build

    Improve detection, attribution, actions, confidence, and evidence. Bluestaq staff are on hand for questions.

  5. Saturday 4:00 pm CT · Deadline

    Submit

    Before 4:00 pm, make your solution repository public and open one pull request with your submission.json. Anything opened or changed later is not accepted. How to submit.

  6. Saturday from 4:00 pm

    Demonstrations and judging

    Five-minute demo plus three minutes of Q&A. Judges score the published 100-point rubric.

  7. After judging

    Results

    The automated diagnostic is posted on the results page, and the judges announce the winner.

The award

Paid summer internship

At Bluestaq headquarters in Colorado Springs. Standard internship eligibility and hiring requirements apply.

Full one-year scholarship

Tuition paid for one year. One winner receives both awards.

Who can compete

NDSU undergraduate students who are U.S. citizens. No prior GNSS experience is needed.
